Privacy Policy

Home / Privacy Policy

At Rapture Therapy Centre, we understand that developmental and pediatric records are deeply personal. We are committed to securing your family's personal details in accordance with the Indian DPDP (Digital Personal Data Protection) Act and clinical medical ethics parameters.

Document Index

  • 1. Information Collected
  • 2. Record Encryption Parameters
  • 3. Clinical Data Collaboration
  • 4. Parent Legal Ownership Rights

1. Information Collected

We collect only the essential personal and developmental details necessary to structure high-quality clinical screenings and schedules:

  • Family Identity: Parent/guardian names, email addresses, and phone numbers.
  • Pediatric Development Data: Child's name, birth age, clinical symptoms, and screening milestone survey answers.
  • Interaction Logs: Free-text contact messages and specific booking times selected.

2. Record Encryption Parameters

Our digital security protocols are structured to ensure absolute compliance and data locking:

  • Transport Encryption: All information sent via our booking forms uses high-end SSL transport layer encryption.
  • Credentialed Access: Only direct consulting clinicians (speech language pathologists, occupational therapists, special educators) possess credentialed access to child history sheets.
  • Anti-Spam Security: We never buy, sell, trade, or distribute your email or phone numbers to third-party marketing firms.

3. Clinical Data Collaboration

Since Rapture Therapy Centre operates in structural cooperation with the Centre for Child Development and SS Occupational Therapy, pediatric logs may be shared internally between consulting specialists only after securing explicit parental consent. This ensures that physical and communication therapies are tightly integrated for your child's benefit.

4. Parent Legal Ownership Rights

As a parent or legal guardian, you retain full ownership of your child's records. You hold the legal right to:

  • Request complete digital copies of all therapy progression reports.
  • Request immediate corrections to phone numbers, ages, or spelling mistakes.
  • Withdraw consent for record sharing between associated occupational therapy or physiotherapy departments.
  • Request complete clinical erasure of your digital data upon completing a child's therapeutic program.
